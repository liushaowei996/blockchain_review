Targeted full-text expansion completed 25 October 2026

Purpose: deepen the OAL synthesis by recovering uncoded original mechanism
candidates and adding directly relevant mechanisms and explicit comparators.
This is an extension of a structured narrative review, not a new systematic
database search or a retrospective PRISMA flow.

Retrieval routes used:
1. Exact-title/DOI checks for all 14 original mechanism candidates outside the
   initial 23-study map. Publisher pages, author/institutional repositories and
   OpenAlex access-location metadata were consulted. Four were fully coded;
   ten remained inaccessible or only partly inspectable. See the individual
   dispositions in expansion-screening.csv.
2. Targeted publisher and citation-following retrieval for maritime AIS message
   authentication (pAIS, SecAIS), receiver/ledger monitoring, UAV hierarchical
   trust and trust-aware routing, underwater signcryption, trust/energy routing,
   and trust-aware federated aggregation. Eight original domain studies were
   inspected and added. Their intended domains and actual settings are separate
   fields in oal-study-register.csv.
3. Full-text inspection of four already cited protocol comparators: PBFT, Raft,
   HotStuff, and Narwhal/Tusk. These form a removable transferable-infrastructure
   subset; they are not counted as maritime deployments.
4. Crossref DOI metadata checking for the eight newly added domain references.
   Previously cited candidates retain stable bibliography keys. The Varshosaz
   key retains 2019, while the citation uses its 2020 journal issue.

Thirty individually checked leads are recorded: 16 included additions, ten
original candidates with incomplete full-text access, and four contextual leads.
The four contextual leads are three reviews and the unavailable exact Sigstore
paper. This register describes these targeted checks; it is not a count of every
search-engine hit encountered. Search ranking and a complete historical list of
discarded web results were not retained. An inaccessible result is not a finding
of scientific ineligibility.

Source versions and successful retrieval URLs are retained in the study register.
Publisher HTML supplied the full Yang et al. article. Publisher PDFs and lawful
author manuscripts supplied the other additions. Supporting section/page locators
and narrow evaluation scopes are attached to every claim. Full texts remain
private and are excluded from Git and the submission archive.

Resulting sample: 39 studies and 89 claim records; 19 intended-domain studies
(11 UAV, 3 maritime AIS, 5 underwater) and 20 transferable studies. The revised
bibliography has 123 entries: 39 numerically coded mechanisms and 84 contextual,
methodological or evaluation references. These denominators serve different roles.
